Put your karaoke files in this folder (subfolders are fine).

Naming convention (used to fill in artist + title):
    Artist - Title.mp4
    Artist - Title.mp3   (+ matching Artist - Title.cdg for MP3+G)

Supported:
    .mp4 / .webm / .m4v        -> video karaoke
    .mp3 + .cdg (same name)    -> classic MP3+G (graphics drawn on the TV)
    .mp3 / .m4a / .ogg / .wav  -> audio only (no on-screen lyrics)

After adding/removing files, click "Rescan songs" on the Admin page
(or just restart the server).
