Melbourne KaraokePlayer
📺 The player runs on the venue laptop
Songs play locally — not in the cloud
The cloud handles requests & the queue. The actual video/audio plays from the laptop running start_agent.bat, which serves the player on your local network.
Open the player

▶ On this laptop

http://localhost:5050/player — open fullscreen on the TV

📡 From another device on the Wi‑Fi

Use the address the agent prints, e.g. http://192.168.x.x:5050/player

Not started yet?
On the laptop, double‑click start_agent.bat. It uploads the song list and prints the player URL. Then open that URL on the TV and tap once to enable playback.
